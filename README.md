# Kaikki on S:ää

**https://ehalmkro.github.io/everything-is-s/** (in Finnish)

A map of every S-group place of business in Finland's 20 biggest cities, and a dashboard of who governs the regional cooperatives that own them.

- **Map** (`site/index.html`): cities shaded by S-group places per 10 000 residents, or by how many cooperative council members sit on the city council; walk-radius zones around every place; the buildings S-group businesses occupy, in 3D up close.
- **Council dashboard** (`site/politics.html`): for the 16 regional cooperatives covering those cities, how many members of each cooperative's elected council (edustajisto) are also municipal councillors, and their parties.

## Method

**S-group places.** Collected in October 2026 from the public S-Etukortti store finder (s-etukortti.fi/toimipaikat), searching each city by name and by every postcode in it. Searching a city name alone misses places whose name doesn't contain the city; the postcode sweep closes that gap, and a cross-check against chain-wide searches found every Prisma, S-market, Sale and Alepa in the 20 cities. 1 743 places. The store finder also lists bonus partners (LähiTapiola, Silmäasema, Hesburger, pharmacies…); they're marked as partners and hidden by default. Places are assigned to municipalities by postcode.

**Buildings.** Each place is matched to the OpenStreetMap building it sits in, or the nearest one within 20 m (60 m for big-box stores often geocoded to their car park). 1 723 of 1 743 places matched, to 1 000 buildings. Heights come from OSM tags where present.

**Cooperative councils.** Member lists come from each cooperative's own website, collected in October 2026: 880 members across 16 cooperatives. Councils are elected in different years (2024–2026), so their terms differ; the dashboard shows each term. Cooperatives don't publish members' party affiliation.

**Municipal politics.** Members were matched to the Ministry of Justice's open candidate data for the 2025 municipal elections, by surname, first name and municipality. Each match was rated by how certain it is (occupations agreeing, how rare the surname is, whether several candidates share the name). Party affiliation comes only from this match. Of 880 members, 467 ran in 2025: 335 were elected councillors and 82 deputy councillors.

**What the data doesn't tell you.**

- Members who didn't run in 2025 count as "didn't run" even if they're politicians, for example MPs who skipped the municipal elections.
- City boards and committees (kaupunginhallitus, lautakunnat), where much zoning work happens, aren't included.
- Name matching is probabilistic: a few matches may be namesakes.
- Overlap is not wrongdoing. Any customer-owner can stand for a cooperative council, and political groups field candidates openly.

## Privacy

Only aggregate counts are published (`site/data/council_summary.json`): per cooperative, and per municipality. No council member is named. The collection and matching code isn't published, since it would rebuild the named lists; the method is described above.
